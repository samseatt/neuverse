# Neuverse Commons

**A commons for developing the organs, charters, and shared intelligence of a more adaptive civilization.**

[neuverse.space](https://neuverse.space/)

Neuverse Commons is an umbrella for work on civilizational systems that can sense more clearly, remember more faithfully, model consequences, deliberate across differences, coordinate without forced uniformity, learn from failure, and repair what their actions disturb.

It begins from a simple premise: civilization can develop a better physiology while remaining answerable to human beings and the living world.

## What belongs here

This repository is the public home for:

- the evolving Neuverse framework and vocabulary;
- the map of its organs and their relationships;
- public charters and constitutional principles;
- shared protocols and cross-organ specifications;
- explanatory notes and public working documents.

The repository will grow incrementally. Its early contents should be read as foundations under active development rather than as a finished institutional design.

## Neuverse, NEURO, and Neusphere

**Neuverse Commons** is the stewardship umbrella and emerging commons.

**NEURO** is the working model of civilizational physiology: a family of organs and edges through which society might improve its capacity for perception, memory, coordination, care, and repair.

**Neusphere** is the proposed cognitive commons within that wider ecology: a distributed human-and-machine-augmented capacity for attention, inquiry, memory, modelling, deliberation, simulation, and collective learning.

The precise boundary between Neuverse and Neusphere remains under development. Their repositories reflect the current division of labour:

- `neuverse` holds shared purpose, charters, governance, the organ map, and cross-organ commitments;
- `neusphere` holds technical and conceptual work specific to the cognitive commons.

## Charters

The charters define what each organ is for, the principles to which it is accountable, and the boundaries within which it may develop. Implementation details may live in separate repositories, but the corresponding public charter belongs here.

Planned structure:

```text
neuverse/
├── README.md
├── charters/
│   ├── README.md
│   ├── c8-constitution.md
│   ├── neusphere.md
│   ├── vitaledge.md
│   └── ...
└── docs/
    └── ...
```

As charters are added, `charters/README.md` will serve as their index. The same source documents may later be rendered as pages on [neuverse.space](https://neuverse.space/).
